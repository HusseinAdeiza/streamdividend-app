How it works

Your position is a program-derived account you control. The vault only ever moves what you put in, plus the dividends it pays out.

01

Deposit

Send AAPLx to the vault. It is minted for you 1:1 as shares — the vault holds the tokens, you hold the shares.

02

Dividend accrues

When Apple pays, the vault converts it to USDC and applies it pro-rata across every share. Your dividends per share rise the same second.

03

Claim or compound

Take your accrued USDC any time, or leave it in. When you withdraw, you are paid out first, then your AAPLx back, share for share.
